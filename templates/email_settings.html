Gmail SMTP Requirement: Google does not accept standard Google account passwords for SMTP. You must use a 16-character App Password:
  1. Enable 2-Step Verification on your Google Account.

  4. Paste the 16-character password into the Password field below.

How it works
  • When a student's attendance drops below {{ settings.attendance_threshold or 75 }}%, they are marked as a Defaulter.
  • If auto-notify is enabled, a warning email is sent after attendance is taken for that course (once per day per student/course).
  • Emails include Student Name, Attendance %, and a Warning Message.
  • Students need an email address on their profile to receive messages.
